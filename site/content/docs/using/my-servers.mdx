title: "Your own MCP servers"
description: "Add a remote MCP server of your own under My profile. Its tools are yours alone, in the written chat, never by voice."

An MCP server brings a service's own tools: Notion's pages, Linear's
issues, your company's wiki, anything that speaks MCP. The household's
servers are under Settings → Add-ons and everyone uses them. Your own go
under **My profile → MCP servers**, and nobody else sees them or their
tools.

Your servers' tools are only for the **written chat** in the app. They are
never offered to a satellite, never in a spoken request, and never in
the household's automations. Name the server in your message, by the
name you gave it, and Niles brings its tools along: "find my notes on the
trip in Notion", "what's assigned to me in Linear?". With Claude or
ChatGPT answering the chat, every one of your tools is at hand anyway.

## Adding a server

Press **Add a server**, give it a name, and paste its address. The
address must start with `https://` and be on the public internet: Niles
won't reach the house's own network, its cluster or itself this way.
Then choose how it signs in.

- **With a key.** Paste the key or token the service gave you. It goes in
  the `Authorization: Bearer` header unless you name another, such as
  `X-API-Key`. You can't read a key back, here or anywhere else; leave
  the field empty when you edit the server to keep it.
- **Sign in on its page.** For a server that wants you to sign in, as
  Notion's and Linear's do. After you add it, your browser goes to the
  server's own sign-in page, and comes back to My profile when you're
  done. Niles keeps what that sign-in gives it, encrypted, and keeps it
  fresh. **Sign in again** starts over, for instance after you revoked
  Niles's access on the service.

Each server shows whether it's connected, and why not when it isn't.
**Reconnect** tries again.

## What each tool may do

Once a server is connected, its tools are listed, each set to **On**,
**Ask** or **Off**.

- A tool the server says only reads starts **On**: Niles uses it without
  asking.
- Every other tool starts at **Ask**: when Niles wants to use it, the
  chat shows a card, and nothing happens until you press **Allow once**.
  See [Approvals](/docs/using/approvals).
- **Off** keeps the tool from Niles altogether.

## What stays private

- Keys and sign-ins are encrypted for you and that server alone, so they
  can't be opened as anyone else's. They never reach the model, a reply,
  a log or the chat, and Niles takes them out of anything a tool sends
  back.
- What a tool answers is information for Niles, never an instruction to
  it.
- Niles's log notes that one of your tools answered, and how much, but
  never what it said, and the same goes for the reply that used it.

Your own servers need Niles's database and its secrets key. Without
them, the page says so.
