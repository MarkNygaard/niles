A schedule is a task Niles does for you again and again: "every Monday
at 8, my open Linear issues by priority", "each weekday at 7, what's in
my calendar today". At its time, Claude or ChatGPT answers it as you, with
the tools you have in the chat: the household's, your mail and calendars,
Strava, your Vault's logins and [your own MCP servers](/docs/using/my-servers).
The answer lands as a new conversation in your chat, named after the
schedule, and you get a notification.

Schedules are yours alone. They are under **My profile → Schedules**,
and nobody else sees them, changes them or runs them. They are never
spoken on a satellite. For the house's lights and devices, use
[automations](/docs/using/automations): those are the household's,
and run without a model.

## Making one

**In My profile.** Press **Add a schedule**, give it a name, write what
Niles should do, and pick when:

- every day at a time,
- weekdays (Monday to Friday) at a time,
- chosen days of the week at a time,
- or every 1, 2, 3, 4, 6, 8 or 12 hours, on the hour from midnight.

Times are in the household's time zone and follow daylight saving. A
schedule runs once an hour at most, since every run is the assistant's
time.

**In the chat.** Say it the way you would to a person: "every Monday at
8, give me my open Linear issues by priority". Niles shows a card with
the name, what it will do and when; nothing is kept until you press
**Allow once**.

Write the instruction so it stands on its own. Each run starts a fresh
conversation and doesn't remember the one the schedule was made in.

## Each schedule

- **Run now** runs it once, straight away, without moving its next time.
- **Pause** stops it until you **Resume** it; it then runs at its next
  time, not the ones it skipped.
- **Edit** changes its name, instruction or time.
- **Runs** lists its latest runs: when each was due, and whether it was
  done, is waiting for you, failed or was missed. **Open** goes to the
  run's conversation.
- **Remove** deletes it and its runs. The conversations it started stay
  in your chat.

## When a tool asks first

A tool that asks before it acts (see [Approvals](/docs/using/approvals))
asks in a schedule too. The run puts its card in the conversation and
waits for you rather than guess, and the run reads **Waiting for your
yes**. The card lapses as any card does if nobody answers.

## Acting on a site without asking

A schedule can be allowed to act on one of your saved logins' sites
without asking: a weekly class booking that opens at midnight, while you
sleep. Pick the login under **May act without asking on** when you add
or edit it, or name the login when you ask for the schedule in the chat;
the chat's card then says which login and what the schedule may do there.
**Nothing, ask each time** takes the permission off.

On that login's sites its runs sign in with that login, click, pick
options and submit forms with no card. Typing, other sites, paying and
ordering still wait on their cards. Each step it takes goes into
Approvals' history as **Allowed by its schedule**.
[Browsing](/docs/using/browsing#a-schedule-that-books-on-its-own) says
exactly what is covered.

The schedule's list shows the login it may act on. A login removed from
your Vault allows nothing any more.

## When a run doesn't happen

- **Missed.** If Niles wasn't running at a schedule's time, the run is
  marked missed and isn't caught up later. The next one runs on time.
- **Failed.** The run says why, in its notification and in its runs:
  no assistant switched on, the assistant couldn't answer, or you're no
  longer on Settings → People.

Schedules need Niles's database, and Claude or ChatGPT switched on under
Settings → Voice \& AI. Without an assistant, the page says so and
nothing runs.
